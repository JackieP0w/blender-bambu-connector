# 🎀 JackieP0w's Kawaii Slicer Connector 🎀

**Author:** JackieP0w  
**Version:** 1.8 (Kawaii Edition ✨)  
**Compatibility:** Blender 3.6+ | Windows & macOS  

Hello, fellow maker! (〃＾▽＾〃) This is a super sweet Blender add-on designed to teleport your 3D models directly into **Bambu Studio** or **OrcaSlicer** with just one click! No more messy exporting and dragging files—let the magic do the work!

✨ Magical Features
One-Click Teleportation: Send selected or visible objects to your slicer instantly! 🚀

Auto-Sizing Hugs: Automatically scales your models by 1000x so they arrive in the slicer at the perfect size—no more "Object too small" warnings! ｡◕‿◕｡

Universal Friendship: Perfectly compatible with both Windows and macOS.

Slicer Swapping: While best friends with Bambu Studio, you can easily change the path in settings to use OrcaSlicer or any other supported slicer! 🔄

3MF Sparkles: Uses the .3mf format to keep your object names and separate parts organized and happy.

Kawaii Inside: Features custom ASCII art and friendly messages to make your 3D modeling sessions more cheerful! (✿◡‿◡)

🐾 Installation
Download: Save the bambu_connector.py file to your computer.

Open Blender: Go to Edit > Preferences > Add-ons.

Install: Click the Install... button at the top right and select your bambu_connector.py file.

Enable: Find "Bambu Connector: Kawaii Edition" in the list and check the box to enable it.

Configure: Expand the add-on settings and paste your Slicer Executable Path (see the tips below!).

🦄 Pro Tip: The "One Window" Secret
To keep your workspace tidy and prevent a million slicer windows from popping up:

Open Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer.

Go to Preferences (Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on Mac).

Look for the box that says "Keep only one Bambu Studio instance" and check it!

Now, every time you click "Send," it will update your current window like magic! (｡♥‿♥｡)

🍏 macOS Friends
When setting your path on a Mac, remember that you have to peek inside the app bundle! You cannot just select the .app icon. Your path should look like this:
/Applications/BambuStudio.app/Contents/MacOS/BambuStudio

How to find it:

Right-click BambuStudio.app in your Applications folder.

Select Show Package Contents.

Navigate to Contents > MacOS.

Copy the path for the file named BambuStudio.

🪟 Windows Friends
Your path is usually the direct link to the .exe file, typically found in your Program Files:
C:\Program Files\Bambu Studio\bambu-studio.exe

🛠 Usage
Open the N-Panel in the 3D Viewport (Press N on your keyboard).

Find the 3D Print tab.

Select your objects and click Send Selected 🎀.

Or hit Send All Visible 🌸 to send your whole unhidden scene at once!
